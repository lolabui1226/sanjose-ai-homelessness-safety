# Homelessness Safety: AI for sorting reports to City Departments

Client: City of San José, San José residents
Team: Lola Bui, Sri Sudula, Juan Chavez, Benjamin Lok, Aug–Oct 2026
My part: I wrote the feasibility analysis and drafted the presentation slides.

## The problem
A person experiencing homelessness in San José, needs a clearer way to report issues to the City and receive follow-up because the current system makes it difficult to know where to report problems or what happens afterward.
The current system fails when they reports a problem but does not know which City department will handle it or what happens next.

## What we built
- Our mobile app, CivicClear, helps residents report sidewalk and public-safety concerns, then uses AI to organize the information and flag missing details for City staff to review.
- The user will able to see the reports that they made and its status, or the City department use can see all reports that are sorted by the urgency.
- The City will still get to read a summarized version of the report and can make a decision on when to take action or what they need to do to take action.

## What I would do next
One gap that I found was that the app would only sort out reports to the City department. For example, if there was a issue that involves a cleanup, I would add a function where the AI would send that report to a clean up agency, or have it reach out to other teams that specialize in homelessness support in general.
